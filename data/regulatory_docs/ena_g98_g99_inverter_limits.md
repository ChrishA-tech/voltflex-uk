# ENA Engineering Recommendations G98 & G99 Inverter & Export Limits

## [ENA-G98-01] ENA Engineering Recommendation G98 Single-Phase Inverter Limit
Authority: Energy Networks Association / Ofgem

Under ENA Engineering Recommendation G98, micro-generators (solar PV, home batteries, V2G) with an aggregate inverter capacity up to 3.68 kW (16 A per phase at 230 V nominal single-phase) may connect under the connect-and-notify fast-track procedure, notifying the DNO within 28 days of commissioning. Exceeding 3.68 kW single-phase export without prior DNO approval is prohibited.

## [ENA-G99-01] ENA Engineering Recommendation G99 Pre-Approval & Thermal Limits
Authority: Energy Networks Association / UK DNOs

Installations with aggregate export or inverter rating above 3.68 kW single-phase (16 A) require ENA Engineering Recommendation G99 application and formal DNO approval before commissioning. DNOs may issue export limitation schemes (G100) or curtail export when substation transformer headroom is constrained or thermal current limits are approached.
