# NESO Demand Flexibility Service (DFS) Statutory Guidance

## [NESO-DFS-01] NESO Demand Flexibility Service (DFS) Participation & Baselines
Authority: National Energy System Operator (NESO)

The NESO Demand Flexibility Service (DFS) rewards registered domestic and I&C assets via approved providers for shifting demand or exporting battery/EV energy during peak system stress windows. Settlement uses half-hourly smart meter readings (MPAN) against a counterfactual baseline; assets must not double-count capacity already committed to the Balancing Mechanism or Capacity Market.

## [NESO-DFS-02] NESO DFS Dispatch Timing & Dynamic Utilization
Authority: National Energy System Operator (NESO)

DFS Test and Live events notify providers ahead of target settlement periods (typically day-ahead or within-day). During Dunkelflaute peaks with low wind generation and high wholesale prices, home batteries and V2G-capable EVs can discharge up to their approved G98 (3.68 kW) or G99 limit.
